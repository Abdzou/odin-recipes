Lasagna recipe

Description

lasagna is well known by garfield readers. It is garfield's prefered dish for a good reason.Lasagna is tasty, rich in protein and rich in flavor.
Follow me and enjoy this lasagna with me

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • garlic
  • Tomato
  • Sugar
  • Spices and Seasonings
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Cheese
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Boil salted water and cook the lasagna noodles according to the package. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cook the Italian sausage, ground beef, and diced onion over medium heat for 8–10 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute. Drain the meat.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, water, sugar, parsley, basil, fennel, salt, and pepper.
  5. Bring the sauce to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Mix the egg, ricotta, remaining parsley, and salt in a bowl.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  8. Spread 2 cups of meat sauce in the bottom of a 13×9-inch baking dish.
  9. Add 3 lasagna noodles.
  10. Spread ⅓ of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  11. Add 1 cup mozzarella and 2 tablespoons Parmesan.
  12. Repeat the layers 2 more times.
  13. Finish with the remaining meat sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan.
  14. Cover and bake for 25 minutes.
  15. Uncover and bake for another 25 minutes.
  16. Remove from the oven and let rest for 15 minutes.
  17. Slice and serve.
